01 — Scope

What “basics” means here

Everything that makes it feel like the Bible App, and nothing that needs a server.

In the MVP

  • Read any chapter, book by book, with verse numbers
  • Verse of the Day on Home, with Continue Reading
  • Highlights, bookmarks and notes — saved on the device
  • Search the full Bible text
  • Reading plans with a daily devotional flow — including 2 yearly through-the-Bible tracks
  • Dark mode, font size, default translation
  • Chapter audio streamed from the open LibriVox KJV recording

Out for now

  • Accounts and cloud sync between devices
  • Audio downloads and text-to-speech (streaming only)
  • Social features: friends, comments, community
  • Paid content, subscriptions, ads
  • Verse image / share-graphics generator
  • Live events and church integrations

Which translations?
KJV only — public domain, bundled inside the app as JSON. No licensing, no API, fully offline.

App language of the interface?
English UI and English KJV text throughout.

Login / accounts?
No login in the MVP. Everything saved on the device (AsyncStorage/MMKV). Sync is a v2 add-on.

Verse images (share graphics)?
Out of MVP. Share action copies the verse text only.

Reading plans content?
5 bundled plans — 3 short samples (7-day Psalms, 21-day Gospels, 5-day Proverbs) plus 2 yearly plans: 365-day through-the-Bible tracks, one in canonical order and one blending OT + NT daily.

Dark mode?
Yes — manual toggle in S-10, plus “follow system”.
—
Audio Bible?
In — streamed from the open LibriVox KJV recording (public domain), one chapter at a time from a play control in the reader. Nothing bundled, nothing downloaded.
—
Visual identity of the app itself?
Warm paper + oxblood + gold, serif reading face — close to YouVersion’s warmth but not a copy.

3

Verse actions

S-05 sheet: highlights, bookmarks, notes persisted locally and rendered in the text.

4

Search

S-06 full-text search with snippets and jump-to-verse.

5

Plans

S-07 → S-08 → S-09 flow with progress tracking, passage links, and the 2 yearly plans.

6

Polish

S-10 settings, dark mode, LibriVox chapter audio in the reader, Home feed assembly, app icon and store-ready build.
